Collin Lucken

Hastings Postdoctoral Scholar in AI & Humanity

Bowdoin College

I am a philosopher studying complex cognitive systems and artificial intelligence engineering through the lens of maker’s knowledge. As Hastings Postdoctoral Scholar in AI and Humanity at Bowdoin College, I also help students and faculty decide how, and whether, to bring AI into their own teaching and research.
